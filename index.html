The venue

Two spaces, one address off Ihiala Street.

Set back behind Government Secondary School in Area 2, Garki, the compound opens into a landscaped garden with genuine room to spread out — chairs, canopies, a dance floor, whatever the day calls for. When the event needs four walls instead, the indoor hall next door is ready, decorated and set for formal occasions.

Past guests describe the grounds as clean and well put together — the kind of backdrop that does a good part of the decorating for you before a single flower arrangement goes up.
